# Free drawing

A whiteboard where you can make pictures from words, or circle one spot on a picture and change only that.

## Pictures from words

Describe the scene in the chat and the picture is placed on the whiteboard. You can pick the size and quality. Write your preferred style and composition in Settings → Memory → Free drawing and they come along every time.

## Circle it, change it

This is Free drawing's everyday way of working:

1. Select a picture;
2. With the pen, circle the spot to change, draw an arrow, or write a few words beside it;
3. Say "put a potted plant here" in the chat, or say nothing and let the marks speak.

The edited picture is placed beside the original, which stays as it was.

> [!WHY]
> Describing "the second cup in the bottom left" in words is tiring and easy to misread, while a circle is hard to get wrong. So the marks are first read into one clear sentence and only then handed to the image model. That sentence is shown to you as well: if the edit goes wrong, you can see where the reading went astray.

## Versions

Every version edited from the same picture is kept together, and the version strip on the canvas switches between them for comparison. Versions never overwrite each other: each edit is a new picture.

## Uploading and arranging

Drag your own pictures onto the whiteboard, as references or as things to edit. The whiteboard is Excalidraw, so shapes, text and arrows are all yours to use.

How it works is in [Editing by annotation](/docs/canvas-edit).
