# Works and saving

What is on screen is what is kept. There is no Save to remember and no reload to fear: as with a chat on claude.ai, every bit you make is already kept.

## My works

My works, at the top, lists everything under All / Diagram / Interior design / Slides / Free canvas. Works can be opened, renamed, duplicated and deleted. Opening a work from another canvas switches straight to it.

A work appears in the list on its own as soon as it has content; an empty draft left without a mark on it is dropped, so no trail of "Untitled" builds up.

## Clearing

Clearing a conversation asks first (you can turn that off in Settings → General). Works already listed stay in My works, and the canvas moves on to a fresh, empty work.

## Backup and moving

Works live in your browser's own IndexedDB and do not follow you to another browser or computer. In Settings → Data you can:

- **Export all works**: one JSON file holding every work of all four canvases, along with their small settings from localStorage;
- **Import**: choose a file you exported before; after you confirm, it replaces all current works and the page reloads.

> [!TIP]
> Export a backup before you clear your browser's data.

The storage underneath is described in [How works are stored](/docs/works-storage).
