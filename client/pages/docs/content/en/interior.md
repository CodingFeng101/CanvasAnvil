# Interior design

Describe the home you want, settle the scheme first, then get a floor plan you can keep working on by hand, and the full drawing set exported from it.

## Where to start

- **From a sentence**: "Three bedrooms, two living rooms, 100 m², windows front and back, a walk-in wardrobe off the main bedroom, warm natural wood."
- **From an existing plan**: upload a photo, screenshot, PDF or DXF. Walls, doors and windows are recognised, and the original stays as an underlay.

## The scheme comes first

The AI does not rush to draw. It first writes a design scheme (rooms, areas, orientation, quiet and active zones, the entrance route), shown step by step in the conversation.

The scheme ends with every **pending choice** it can settle, in six groups: layout, finishes, equipment, appliances, smart home, and electrics and plumbing. Kitchen shape, each room's floor, wall colours, ceilings, air conditioning, fresh air, underfloor heating, number of sockets, where the panels go… Each has a recommended value already selected. Click one to change it, confirm, and only then is the plan generated.

> [!WHY]
> Most disagreements in a renovation are about details. Laying those choices out to click is far less work than drawing first and revising round after round, and it hands the model requirements you confirmed instead of its own guesses.

## The house designer

The plan opens in the designer built into the canvas: 2D plan, 3D bird's-eye view and walkthrough, furniture, electrics and plumbing, ceilings, flooring, and a demolition layer. Every wall and every piece of furniture can be dragged and edited directly.

## Finishing touches and the audit

Once the plan is generated, a few things happen on their own:

1. Furniture that sits in a wall or blocks a curtain is pushed clear;
2. Electrical and plumbing points are placed in one go, then adjusted to the preferences you chose;
3. The designer's self-check runs, along with a **room-by-room electrical audit**: which switch works which light, mounting heights, whether a switch by a door is on the handle side, two-way switches in bedrooms, the gas alarm in the kitchen, splash-proof sockets in bathrooms…

The audit only reports; it never moves points on its own. Problems are listed in the conversation, and you can fix them under the designer's electrics panel or ask the AI to.

## Editing by conversation, and history

"Make the main bedroom a bit bigger", "add a window to the balcony": the AI edits with the recent conversation in mind and says what it changed, or why it did not. The history button lists every version as a thumbnail (AI generated, edited by hand, imported from a drawing). Any of them can be restored, and a restore can itself be undone.

## Delivery

The quote, A3 drawing set PDF, DXF (layout, electrical points, demolition), colour plan and 3D renders all export from Quote and Export drawings at the top right of the designer. When the server has a browser, a Delivery check can run as well.
